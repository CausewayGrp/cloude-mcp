The laws the estate obeys

Permission follows position, never person. What you can open is decided by the role you hold, not your name (POL-GOV-03).
A form is a data contract, not a document. Every field typed, required flags set, RBAC read strictly — a D0 field is never seen operationally.
Nothing advances silently. Every step updates the row, appends to the immutable AuditLog, and notifies the next holder.
The screening gate sits before the sale. SCR-DECLINE is terminal and synchronous, routed to the MD on every channel. A decline costs a conversation, not a commitment.
English primary; Arabic native. AR content is native-separate, never machine-mixed in one surface (POL-LANG-01).
The rating system is built last. People & Culture completes at the Z08 finale, where every JD and KPI combines.
